## Full Description
Stellar Limbo by JILLU is an exhilarating, fast-paced multiplier arcade game designed for thrill-seekers. Unlike traditional slots, players take control of their destiny by predicting the rocket's breaking point. 

**Gameplay Overview:**
Enter your bet and choose a Target Multiplier. As the rocket launches, the multiplier rapidly increases. If the final crash multiplier meets or exceeds your target, you win the payout instantly. If the rocket explodes before hitting your target, the bet is lost. 

**Features:**
- Seamless, lag-free 60FPS rocket animations.
- Dynamic Auto-betting system (Martingale, Stop-Loss, Target Profit).
- Integrated Provably Fair mechanics allowing players to independently verify the randomness of every round.
- Ultra-responsive mobile UI engineered for one-handed portrait gameplay.
- Procedural audio engine for immersive sound effects.
